## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-28

### Added

- Menu bar toggle for `pmset disablesleep`, with a distinct icon for each state.
- Icon sync with changes made outside the app, checked every 30 seconds.
- Open at Login option backed by `SMAppService`, enabled on first launch.
- Universal build for Apple silicon and Intel Macs.
- `make install`, `make sudoers`, `make uninstall` and `make dist` targets.
